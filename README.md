## Dashboard

The Power BI dashboard is organized into four analytical pages covering the Executive Overview, Customer & Product Analysis, Logistics & Delivery, and Customer Satisfaction, complete with corresponding image links (`Images/Executive_Overview.png`, `Images/Customer_Product_Analysis.png`, `Images/Logistics_Delivery.png`, and `Images/Customer_Satisfaction.png`).

## Key Insights

The analysis covers 99,441 unique orders with verified revenue reconciliation. Key findings include an average delivery time of ~12 days (7.65% late rate), low repeat customer rates (6.16% vs 93.84% one-time), an average review score of 4.0/5 linked to delivery performance, and geographic delivery variations.

## Recommendations

* **Improve Delivery Performance:** Investigate high late-delivery states.
* **Increase Customer Retention:** Target one-time purchasers with loyalty initiatives.
* **Proactive Monitoring & KPIs:** Track delayed orders and maintain continuous KPI observation.
